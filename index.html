EXPLAINABLE MATCHING

A wider lens on relevance.

School influence rarely fits one job title. The ranker looks for shared professional context and makes its evidence visible.

01

Analyze context

Normalize the bio into role, school operations, ecosystem reach, and experience concepts. Curated synonyms connect related terms like “school group” and “multi-campus network.”

02

Compare profiles

TF-IDF vectors and cosine similarity compare weighted context across profiles. An education-domain concept map adds semantic-like matches beyond exact keyword overlap.

03

Explain & rank

A weighted score combines contextual similarity (55%), concept overlap (25%), role fit (12%), and location (8% when available). Each result shows matched signals and source links.
